# DDS Chapter 07
# Agreement Domain

**Document ID:** DDS-07

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

---

# Purpose

The Agreement Domain manages all occupancy agreements generated by TenoPilot.

An Agreement represents the legal document governing an occupant's stay within a Property.

Agreements are automatically generated using operational information already stored within the system.

The Agreement itself should never become the primary source of business data.

---

# Core Philosophy

Occupant information owns the data.

The Agreement presents that data.

Changing an Agreement should never directly modify business records.

Operational data should always remain the source of truth.

---

# Agreement Types

The system currently supports two agreement types.

• Tenant Agreement

• Guest Stay Agreement

Future agreement types should extend the existing model.

---

# Tenant Agreement

Generated for long-term occupants.

Typical agreement information includes:

• Occupant Information

• Property Information

• Room & Bed Allocation

• Monthly Rent

• Security Deposit

• Joining Date

• Notice Terms

• Organization Details

• Terms & Conditions

---

# Guest Agreement

Generated for short-term occupants.

Typical agreement information includes:

• Guest Information

• Property Information

• Room & Bed Allocation

• Check-in Date

• Check-out Date

• Stay Charges

• Organization Details

• Terms & Conditions

---

# Agreement Generation

Agreements are generated automatically using information collected during onboarding.

The system assembles information from:

Occupant

↓

Property

↓

Room Allocation

↓

Organization

↓

Financial Information

↓

Agreement

Manual document preparation should never be required.

---

# Agreement Review

Before an agreement becomes active, users review the generated document.

The review process allows users to verify:

• Personal Information

• Property Information

• Financial Information

• Accommodation Details

The final confirmation action is:

Agree & Continue

Only after confirmation should the occupant profile become active.

---

# Agreement Ownership

Every Agreement belongs to:

One Organization

↓

One Property

↓

One Occupant

An Agreement cannot exist without an Occupant.

---

# Agreement Versioning

Historical agreements should always remain preserved.

Whenever a significant change requires a new agreement, the previous version should remain available.

Examples

Room Transfer

Rent Revision

Agreement Renewal

Historical agreements should never be overwritten.

---

# Agreement Lifecycle

Agreement Generated

↓

Agreement Reviewed

↓

Agreement Accepted

↓

Active

↓

Archived

Archived agreements remain available for future reference.

---

# Agreement Relationship

An Agreement references existing business information.

It does not own:

• Occupant Identity

• Property Information

• Room Information

• Financial Information

Those remain owned by their respective domains.

---

# Digital Acceptance

The current workflow requires user confirmation through:

Agree & Continue

Future versions may support:

• Digital Signature

• Aadhaar Verification

• OTP Verification

• Electronic Signature Providers

These features should extend the Agreement Domain without redesign.

---

# Agreement Storage

The generated agreement should remain accessible from the Occupant Profile.

Owners should be able to:

• View Agreement

• Download Agreement

• Print Agreement

• View Previous Versions

Agreement access should remain simple throughout the occupant lifecycle.

---

# Agreement Updates

Operational changes should not automatically modify historical agreements.

Examples

Updating Phone Number

Changing Emergency Contact

Uploading Aadhaar

These updates should affect the Occupant Profile only.

A new agreement should be generated only when legally significant information changes.

---

# Future Expansion

The Agreement Domain should support:

• Renewal Agreements

• Amendment Agreements

• Extension Agreements

• Multi-language Agreements

• Property-specific Templates

Future agreement types should extend the existing model.

---

# Design Principles

Agreements are generated from operational data.

Operational data remains the source of truth.

Historical agreements should always be preserved.

Every agreement belongs to one Occupant.

Agreement generation should remain automatic.

Users should review before acceptance.

Future legal enhancements should extend rather than replace the existing agreement model.
