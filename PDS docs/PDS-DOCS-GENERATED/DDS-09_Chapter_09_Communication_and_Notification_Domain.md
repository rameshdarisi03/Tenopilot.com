# DDS Chapter 09
# Communication & Notification Domain

**Document ID:** DDS-09

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

---

# Purpose

The Communication & Notification Domain manages all outbound communication generated by TenoPilot.

The domain delivers business notifications without owning business data.

Communication should always be triggered by operational events occurring in other domains.

The Communication Domain should remain independent while integrating with Occupants, Financial, Maintenance and Reporting domains.

---

# Core Philosophy

Business modules create communication events.

The Communication Domain delivers them.

Communication should never become the source of operational data.

---

# Scope

The Communication Domain manages:

• WhatsApp Messages

• Email Notifications (Future)

• SMS Notifications (Future)

• In-App Notifications

• Bulk Communication

• Reminder Scheduling

• Notification Templates

• Delivery History

Future communication channels should extend this domain.

---

# Communication Ownership

Every communication belongs to:

One Organization

↓

One Property

↓

One Recipient

or

↓

Multiple Recipients

Communication records should never exist independently.

---

# Communication Sources

Communication may originate from:

Occupant Management

Financial Domain

Maintenance Domain

Organization Settings

System Automation

Future Modules

The originating business module remains the owner of the business event.

---

# Supported Recipients

Communication may target:

Single Occupant

Multiple Occupants

Property Staff

Owners

Managers

Receptionists

Recipient selection depends on the originating business event.

---

# Bulk Communication

The system supports bulk communication.

Recipients may be selected individually, by property, or by occupant lifecycle state.
